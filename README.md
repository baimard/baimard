I'm a French cybersecurity consultant and open-source developer.

Today, most of my work is dedicated to **Gestion**, an invoicing and electronic invoicing application for Nextcloud.

---

# 🚀 Featured Project

## Gestion

**Gestion** is an open-source invoicing application designed for freelancers, micro-businesses and small companies.

It helps users manage their business while keeping complete ownership of their data inside Nextcloud.

### Features

- 📄 PDF quotations and invoices
- 🧾 Factur-X electronic invoices
- 👥 Customer management
- 📦 Product catalog
- 📧 Email sending
- ☁️ Secure storage inside Nextcloud
- 🇪🇺 Electronic invoicing ready

👉 https://github.com/baimard/gestion
